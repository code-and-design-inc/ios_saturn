[en] English
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Promotional Text:
The ultimate Sega Saturn emulator! Fixes black screens, freezes, flicker and audio dropouts in Die Hard Trilogy, Panzer Dragoon Saga, Sonic 3D Blast and more.

What's New:
- DIE HARD TRILOGY / LAYER SECTION II: fixed the screen going completely black
- PANZER DRAGOON SAGA / GUNGRIFFON II: fixed a freeze during gameplay
- SONIC JAM: fixed low frame rate; ASTAL / STREET FIGHTER ALPHA 3: fixed flicker
- SONIC 3D BLAST: fixed the opening movie stalling and characters hidden by the ground
- Audio: fixed dropouts and crackling, and stabilized the frame rate at 60fps
- MR. BONES: fixed characters not being drawn
- Save states: fixed some data not being saved, improving reliability
- Fixed the app becoming unresponsive when exiting a game
